Best Man

Jason Gervias

Jason and I met years ago and quickly became friends—the kind of friends who feed off each other's energy, which has led to both great ideas and some very questionable ones. Even though life gets busy and we don't talk as often as we used to, we know we're always just a phone call away when the other needs help, and every time we do talk it's like no time has passed. I'm really grateful to have him here today—so everyone please give it up for my best man, Jason!

Maid of Honor

Brenda Maldonado

From concerts, to road trips, to wine tasting and everything in between, Meg can't imagine her life without Brenda. They met at the aquarium and quickly became friends. Within a few months she was telling Meg how perfect her and Kevin are together, and turned into her closest and most supportive friend. Now she gets to fill the very important role of maid of honor, and is throwing Meg a bridal shower in Mexico with all of her family! Meg is so excited to have her best friend by her side on her special day.

Groomsman

I met Eric through Meghan—they're cousins—on a trip to Atlanta, and since then we've spent a lot of time gaming online and catching up about everyday life. He's a great guy and an awesome friend, even if we do give him a hard time from time to time. But in all seriousness, Eric… you still need to work on those Call of Duty skills.

Bridesmaid

Courtney Hyland

Courtney and Meg grew up together and no matter the distance have always stayed close friends. Between line dancing, fishing, and traveling to new places, Meg and Courtney will always be best friends.

Groomsman

Juan Chavez

I met Juan at work, and we quickly became friends. He's the kind of guy who shows up anytime someone needs help without even thinking twice—even when it's something none of us really want to do. He's always pushing us to be our best and is the first person there to listen when we need to vent.

Bridesmaid

Nikki Bower

Nikki is Meg's sister. She is 8 years older than Meg, so they didn't have much in common growing up—they were also complete opposites. As they grew into adults, they began to share more with each other and discover more similarities, especially their love for country music. As they planned Nikki's wedding 10 years ago, they grew a new bond, and now they get to do that all over again. Meg is so grateful to have a caring and supportive sister to stand by her side on her special day.

Groomsman

I met Jon Hanna in the coral industry, and he quickly became both a dependable employee and a good friend. Through all the shifts in the economy, I've always enjoyed working with Jon and watching him grow over the years. Outside of work we've built a real friendship around a lot of shared interests.

Bridesmaid

Tatyuana Williams

Meg and Taty also met at work, and Taty immediately felt comfortable opening up to her. They've been friends ever since, and she fits right in with Meg's crazy group of friends!

Bridesmaid

Amber is Meg's older cousin. No one understands the family dynamic like her. Meg can't wait to make her learn line dances and hang out with her crazy group of friends. She's grateful to have big cousins that are always there for her, and Amber's unwavering support and big-sister energy mean the world (especially since she's always with Meg's big sister). Meg feels so lucky to share this special day with her.
